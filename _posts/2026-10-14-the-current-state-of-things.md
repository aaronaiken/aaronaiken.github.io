## Beta Stuff
Let's get this out of the way. I don't want to obsess over this because that is something I would tend to do. I don't want to sell anything, but I do want people to know about things I'm working on...that's kind of the point with making things, whether the medium is leather or code or words. Share what you make, a mantra I need to begin repeating to myself every day. I have so many awesome things sitting out there, using them daily myself but allowing my nerves, _imposter syndrom_, to influence how much I talk about things I'm making. 

I digress.

If you take a look at my site, on my "[workbench](https://aaronaiken.me/tools/)" page, you'll see a massive list of projects that I have up and running. Most are private to me only, in "dogfood" status. Others are in TestFlight, but still private. Two, [Cloudbase](https://cloudbase.day) and [Still Dark](https://aaronaiken.me/tools/still-dark/), are in open beta for anyone to try. Cloudbase I am limiting to 25 beta seats for now ([grab one here](https://cloudbase.day/signup)). Still Dark, [have at it](https://testflight.apple.com/join/CPjjmV97). 

My current _promote_ focus is to fill the beta seats for Cloudbase so that I can begin getting some solid feedback in from people other than my crazy brain. While that is in that mode, I'm working on another app and will have it in public TestFlight shortly, hopefully next week sometime. I don't have a page for it yet on my site, but it is called _Kitchen Table_ and it is meant for tracking bigger decisions that you (and maybe your partner) have decided on. Kitchen Table will be a shared spot to record what we have decided.

Beta will open, I promise, 2027 for sure. Endpaper is the first app I made and has been the one I am laboring over the most before I get it out to people. Not a good habit, but it is very near and dear to my heart, more so than anything else I have made. So, all of that to say, take a look at the homepage, enjoy it, and if this type of writing/thinking/publishing app feels like you, [add your name to the beta list](https://endpaper.day/#beta).

## AI
Ah, the wonderful world of AI. I've been using AI in my professional work since 2021, certainly ahead of the curve compared to my colleagues, our industry, and even wider cultural acceptance of the technology. I have been using it personally since around that time as-well, first with Chat-GPT, then Gemini, and have fully landed on Claude as my "daily driver". My company provides an enterprise license for that end of things and I have been very happy with Max on my personal account. I have a toolbox filled with AI tools now, all meant for varioius things which they all uniquely excel at. I've also been building them into various tools of my own via APIs - I see nothing but positive outcomes with this technology. 

And that's the point I want to make here. Any technology can be used for bad, that goes without saying, but I think the positive will _always_ outweigh the negative. The _Internet_ is used to accomplish and allow for so much good around the world...but it also has its fair share of the bad and ugly. I'd rather have the Internet than not, and I'd rather have artificial intelligence than not. Like anything else, it is a _tool_ to be utilized. Use it to make your life easier where you can, use it to create, use it to brainstorm, use it to _do_, and I say don't let the naysayers scare you from all that can be accomplished. Most importantly, _have fun!_
