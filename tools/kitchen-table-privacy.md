*Effective: 2026-10-19*

This is the privacy policy for [Kitchen Table](/tools/kitchen-table/), a household decision log for iPhone. It's written in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, [email me](mailto:aaron@aaronaiken.me).

The one-sentence version: **Kitchen Table doesn't collect any data. Everything you write is stored in your own iCloud account, and only you and the people you choose to share with can see it.**

---

## What It Stores

What you write: your decisions (titles, notes, cost, vendor, dates, photos, follow-ups) and your money plan (its steps, targets and notes). There is no name, no email, no account and no profile.

It's stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so it can sync between your devices.

## Sharing

If you share your household, the people you invite can see and edit it. That happens through Apple's iCloud sharing, between your account and theirs. Nothing passes through anything of mine.

## What It Collects

Nothing. There are **no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.** I (Aaron Aiken, the developer) have no server for Kitchen Table and can't access anything you write.

## Your Control

- **Keep a copy** any time, as a file you can save in Files.
- **Remove everything** by deleting the app, then removing its iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Kitchen Table.

## Support

If you contact me for support, through the [support page](/tools/kitchen-table/support/) or by email, I'll use what you send only to reply.

## Children

Kitchen Table collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It is rated 4+.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the change will be stated plainly.
