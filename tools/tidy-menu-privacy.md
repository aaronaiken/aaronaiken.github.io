*Last updated: 2026-09-25*

This is the privacy policy for [TidyMenu](/tools/tidy-menu/), the menu-bar declutter utility. It's written in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, [email me](mailto:aaron@omg.lol).

The one-sentence version: **TidyMenu runs entirely on your Mac, collects nothing, and sends nothing to me or anyone else.**

---

## What It Collects

Nothing. TidyMenu has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, and no server. It makes no network connections of its own — there is nowhere for your data to go, because there is no "away."

---

## What It Stores

Only what it needs to remember your setup between launches: whether your icons are currently collapsed, whether you've seen the one-time welcome, and your launch-at-login preference. These live in your Mac's standard app preferences, on your Mac. They never leave it.

---

## Permissions

TidyMenu asks for **no** system permissions — no accessibility, no screen recording, no automation, nothing. It's sandboxed by the App Store, and it needs none of the escape hatches a sandbox can grant. It arranges its own menu-bar items and that's all.

---

## What It Never Does

- Never collects, stores, or transmits any personal data.
- Never uses analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.
- Never talks to a server — there isn't one.
- Never asks for a permission it doesn't need (which is all of them).

---

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since TidyMenu collects nothing and sends nothing, there isn't much here that *can* change — but if it does, it'll be stated plainly.
