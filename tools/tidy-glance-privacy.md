*Last updated: 2026-09-25*

This is the privacy policy for [TidyGlance](/tools/tidy-glance/), the glanceable menu-bar calendar. It's written in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, [email me](mailto:aaron@omg.lol).

The one-sentence version: **TidyGlance runs entirely on your Mac, collects nothing, reads none of your calendars, and sends nothing anywhere.**

---

## What It Collects

Nothing. TidyGlance has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, and no server. It makes no network connections of its own.

---

## What It Reads

Nothing of yours. TidyGlance draws a month grid from the calendar math built into macOS — it does **not** use EventKit, does **not** read your Calendar app, and has no idea what your events are. It shows the date; it doesn't know your plans.

---

## What It Stores

Only your display preferences — your menu-bar date format, which day the week starts on, and whether to show week numbers — in your Mac's standard app preferences, on your Mac.

---

## Permissions

TidyGlance asks for **no** system permissions at all. No calendar access, no network, nothing. It just draws a grid.

---

## What It Never Does

- Never collects, stores, or transmits any personal data.
- Never reads your calendars or events.
- Never uses analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.
- Never talks to a server — there isn't one.

---

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since TidyGlance collects nothing and sends nothing, there isn't much here that *can* change — but if it does, it'll be stated plainly.
