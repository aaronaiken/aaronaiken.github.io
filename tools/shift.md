<section class="sh-sec">
  <div class="sh-wrap">
    <p class="sh-eyebrow">The one rule</p>
    <p class="sh-quote">Dismissal is never <em>completion.</em></p>
    <p class="sh-body">Every sit-stand reminder fails the same way: it fires a timer, you swipe it away, swiping becomes reflex, and the app is dead in three weeks. Shift has one rule everything else follows from — <b>a nudge resolves only when your iPhone's barometer feels you actually change height.</b> Stand up and it registers the move on its own; when it genuinely can't tell, Shift says so and trusts your tap. A swipe is never counted as done.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="sh-sec" style="padding-top:0;">
  <div class="sh-wrap">
    <p class="sh-eyebrow">The quieter idea</p>
    <h2 class="sh-h2">Standing too long is a failure too.</h2>
    <p class="sh-body">Prolonged static standing is the classic risk factor for varicose veins, so the unit of success isn't standing minutes — it's <b>transitions</b>. In your Trends, a ninety-minute stand shows up in the same red as a ninety-minute sit, and Shift will nudge you back <em>down</em> out of a too-long stand. Your keyboard always still works.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="sh-sec">

    <p class="sh-eyebrow">What it does</p>
    <h2 class="sh-h2">A nudge you can act on, then verify.</h2>
    <div class="sh-how">
      <div>
        <span class="tag">The nudge</span>
        <h3>An actual movement.</h3>
        <p>Not "stand up" — a specific micro-exercise like finger extensions or a wrist stretch, with a plain "Not sure how?" guide a tap away.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <span class="tag">The proof</span>
        <h3>Verified, not dismissed.</h3>
        <p>The barometer confirms the move where it can, and logs each one — "Stood up, felt +2.9&nbsp;m." Honest about the times it has to trust your tap.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <span class="tag">The score</span>
        <h3>Transitions, not minutes.</h3>
        <p>Your day as a sit / stand / move / away bar, a daily transitions target, and a longest-static-block chart that treats sitting and standing alike.</p>
